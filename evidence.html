ARTIFACT A-17

Partially charred bamboo fragment

Accession
19.088
Recovered
2 NOV 2019
Location
Mount Ashland, forest road below summit
Finder
W-0587
Dimensions
L 19.6 cm · Ø 3.1 cm
Report
RVF-2019-031

Hollow culm section with two nodes, burned through at one end. Found beside a small ring of stones that had been swept clean. The unburned end is cut square.

Examination: No bamboo is known to grow wild at this elevation. Ash residue is consistent with a small, hot fire. Species identification pending. Some readers have connected the fragment to the fire in the origin narrative (Layer III). The archive draws no connection.

Drawn from memory by a neighbor who saw the subject at close range. The witness added, unprompted, that the thread “was dark green, not black.”

Examination: The earliest drawn record of Symbol 001 in the collection. Proportions match later specimens closely.

Anonymous note

Location
Lithia Park, information kiosk
Medium
Ink on ruled index card
Folds

Report
RVF-2022-012

Reads, in full: “The fallen alder below the upper footbridge has been moved. The path is open again. Walk softly.” Unsigned. A small mark matching Symbol 001 is drawn at lower right.

Examination: The path had in fact been cleared. Parks staff had no record of the work. Handwriting not compared.

About Collection 07

Collection 07 gathers witness statements, field notes, recovered objects and secondary folklore concerning a recurring figure reported in the Rogue Valley of Southern Oregon. The archive records. It does not verify, endorse, or explain.
