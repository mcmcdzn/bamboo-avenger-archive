Archive noteCreated from recurring descriptions contained in independent reports collected throughout Southern Oregon.

The Bamboo Avenger is a recurring figure reported throughout the Rogue Valley region.

Descriptions vary. Several recurring characteristics remain remarkably consistent.

Observed elements include
  • Bamboo staves
  • Hooded outerwear
  • Forest symbol
  • Trail systems
  • Anonymous acts of service
  • Unexplained disappearances

Where reports cluster

Every report in the collection falls within a narrow band running south from the Plaza, up the Ashland Creek canyon, through Lithia Park and into the watershed, with a separate group on Mount Ashland.

The archive has not established why. Most of these places are reached by trail, and most are near water.

How the record is arranged

Three layers

The collection is kept in three layers. Each step deeper moves further from what was observed and closer to what is told. The archive takes no position on whether the Bamboo Avenger is real.

About Collection 07

Collection 07 gathers witness statements, field notes, recovered objects and secondary folklore concerning a recurring figure reported in the Rogue Valley of Southern Oregon. The archive records. It does not verify, endorse, or explain.

Evidence remains inconclusive.
