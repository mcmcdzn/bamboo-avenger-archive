A single line of Japanese text recorded in reports, sketches and materials associated with the subject.

First recorded 17 OCT 2009Occurrences 9Variants None recordedStatus Open
Phrase 001 · as recorded 森に一本、影が立つ。 Mori ni ippon, kage ga tatsu. In the forest stands a single shadow.
Archive noteThe phrase appears repeatedly in reports, sketches, and materials associated with Bamboo Avenger folklore. Its significance remains uncertain.

About Collection 07

Collection 07 gathers witness statements, field notes, recovered objects and secondary folklore concerning a recurring figure reported in the Rogue Valley of Southern Oregon. The archive records. It does not verify, endorse, or explain.
